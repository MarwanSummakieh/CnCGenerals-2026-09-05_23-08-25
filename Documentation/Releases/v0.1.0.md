The first Windows demo of **Neon Frontier**: a single-player military RTS with two factions, 52 unit and building variants, and a contested river valley.

## Download and play

- **Windows installer:** `NeonFrontier-0.1.0-Windows-Setup.exe`. Installs for your Windows account, adds a Start Menu shortcut, and includes an uninstaller. No administrator access or Unity Editor is required.
- **Portable ZIP:** `NeonFrontier-0.1.0-Windows-x64.zip`. Extract the entire archive and run `NeonFrontier/NeonFrontier.exe`.
- SHA-256 files accompany both downloads. The installer is unsigned, so Windows may display an unknown publisher.

Choose **Skirmish**, select a faction and difficulty, then **Begin Operation**. Destroy the enemy headquarters while protecting your own.

## In this demo

- Pacific Vanguard and Crimson Dynasty, each with 16 mobile unit types and 10 structures.
- Worker construction, supply income, technology prerequisites, production queues and rally points.
- Four-slot airfields with assigned parking pads, aircraft ammunition, return and rearming.
- Factory rail deployment, barracks exits, army upgrades, power management and veteran ranks.
- Fog of war, radar, control groups and a command console with rendered model portraits.
- Traveling shells and missiles, explosions, smoke, debris, dust and mechanical animation.
- Three difficulty settings, pause/options, victory/defeat and rematches.

This is an early single-player skirmish release. Campaigns, multiplayer, saved matches, transport boarding and skeletal infantry animation are not included. Balance remains in development.
